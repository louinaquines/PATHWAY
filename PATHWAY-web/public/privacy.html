PATHWAY Privacy Policy

PATHWAY is an OJT (on-the-job training) management system built as a capstone project for the College of Computer Studies, University of Cebu – Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue.

What we collect

Student and coordinator account details, OJT requirements, attendance, journals, placements, and supervisor evaluation answers entered into the system.

How we use it

Only to run the OJT program: tracking requirements and hours, reviewing submissions, and collecting supervisor evaluations.

Gmail access

PATHWAY uses the Gmail send-only permission (gmail.send) for the system mailbox to send evaluation and notification emails. It cannot read, delete, or manage any inbox. No Gmail data is stored or shared.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is visible only to the student, their assigned coordinator, and authorized school staff.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: pathwaysystem2@gmail.com
